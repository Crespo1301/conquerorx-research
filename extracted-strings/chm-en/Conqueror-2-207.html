12.6 Mandatory Fields

Back Office > FBT > Mandatory Field Setup

Select which member data is mandatory from:

  • Name
  • Street address
  • City
  • State/Province
  • Post code
  • Country
  • At least one phone number
  • At least an e-mail address
  • Privacy options

When saving the member data, the chosen mandatory fields are displayed in yellow and must be completed.

To access this setup, the related Access to the Mandatory Field Setup User Privilege must be enabled on the operator's profile.

Filtering FB without Mandatory Fields Filled

It is possible to search in the member archive which FB have some mandatory fields empty, in order to contact them and to complete their data: enter the data in the Member Search Window, then press on Missing Fields .

Disable Personal Identification Fields

For privacy purposes it is possible to disable all the fields related to the personal identification of the FB, as the gender, the birth date, etc.
