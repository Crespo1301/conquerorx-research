2.3 Character Factory

Aim of the game is to create an avatar.

Starting from a neutral model, the different parts of the character (body, legs, arms, head, pet) will be customized after each frame (5 frames/5 changes).

Picture first frame is bowled, the different types of body are displayed together with the score to bowl in order to have it.

At the end of game, all the characters are displayed together.

At the end of the game, a printout of the bowler's character can be given to bowlers.
