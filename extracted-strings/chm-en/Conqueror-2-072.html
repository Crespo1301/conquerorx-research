5.7 Selling POS Items

Click in the 3 columns next to the appropriate player or the lane to sell the Fast Sale Items set through the Price Setup module.

To sell POS articles, select the players or the lane in the last column and open the POS module through the Lane button, or if an individual bowler is selected, this button displays the player's name. This opens the Sales window in which the desired articles can be selected.

In this case, the player Matthew has been selected and therefore the articles selected in the Sales window will be attributed to him.

This operation can also be performed when modifying/closing a lane.

LANE MANAGEMENT > Opening a Lane > Selling POS Items
