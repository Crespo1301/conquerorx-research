6. Modifiers

Setup > Price Setup > Modifiers

Modifiers are necessary for managing all the changes carried out on price keys (e.g. an extra sauce on a hamburger or a slice of cheese less).

For example, to sell a "hamburger with extra ketchup but without cheese" the operator has to choose the normal "hamburger" price key, the With ketchup modifier and the Without cheese modifier.

During the sales phase it is necessary to specify if the modifier is With or Without .

A With modifier (e.g. extra Ketchup) can involve a supplement (a price added to the one of the normal price key). A Without modifier does not imply a discount on the price of the normal price key.

Duplicating Groups of Modifiers

The same group of modifiers could be used in different ways. As an example, the modifier "Sauce" could be either free (with hamburgers) or not (with fries). In order to expedite the creation of duplicated groups of modifiers, it can be copied/pasted. In this case, doing so allows linking the first group of modifiers to hamburgers and set prices to zero and to link the duplicated group of modifiers to fries and assign a price.
