30. Advertising - Universal

AccuScore, AS 80/90, Frameworx
Front Desk > Lane Control > Advertising

Press New Category to create a new category and to give it an appropriate name, such as "Restaurant Advertising" or "Saturday Night Packages".

To fill in the category with the desired images, press Add Image . The image chosen and loaded will be pre-displayed as it will be shown on the lanes. The images loaded into Conqueror Pro must be in Bitmap, Jpg or Gif format, 256 colors, size 640 (width) x 480 (height) pixels. If the images are smaller they will be enlarged on the screen, while if the images are bigger they will be minimized. Press Delete Image to delete an image from the present category.

To remove all the images from the present category, press Empty Category while to delete a category with all the images it contains, press Delete Category . To modify the name of the category, press Rename .

Before modifying the Advertising Categories it is advisable to press Refresh , to quickly synchronize the terminal with the server.

After selecting all the images and setting their display order, choose for how many seconds every single image of the sequence has to be shown (Display Time ).

Press Save to save the sequence and Send to MMS to send the sequence in progress to the MMS screen.
