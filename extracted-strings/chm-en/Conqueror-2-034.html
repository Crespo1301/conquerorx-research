5. Payments

5.1 Payment Window

The Payment window provides a handy and versatile tool that allows various financial operations: a transaction can be paid, paid with receipt, split into two or more transactions and different Payment Modes can be managed.

This window contains a table with a different row for each item sold and the fields above the table are:

  • Total that indicates the total amount of the current bill including taxes and discounts; by clicking on this box the Tax Details window opens, displaying the net total, the different taxes and the receipt total.
  • Total Collected that indicates the amount already collected.
  • Still Due that indicates the amount still due if the customer has only paid a part of the total amount using Special Payments; this amount can be paid using another special payment, or cash.
  • Amount Tendered that indicates the amount the customer is paying; by entering an amount here, the Change Due is automatically calculated.
  • Points that indicates how many points are to be assigned to an FB. Click on this box to select the member to whom the points collected through the Players Club feature will be assigned.
  • Tax-exempt ID that opens a window where the customer's tax-exempt ID can be entered. When a transaction is marked as Tax-exempt , all add-on taxes are removed, and the balance adjusted.
  • Payment Modes that indicates the amount paid through Special Payment.

The Pay button allows immediate payment collection, without printing a receipt; to collect the payment and print the receipt, press Receipt . Should the printout fail for any reason, to reprint the receipt, enter POS > Reprint Receipts.

During the payment phase, it is also possible to link the current bill to another one previously saved by clicking on the Link Tab : select the tab to be paid together with the current one, and press Choose .

A bill can be split into two or more different bills, allowing the customer to pay at different stages, or allowing different customers to pay part of the whole amount (e.g. if members of a group decide to have separate bills). To split the bill in parts press Split .
