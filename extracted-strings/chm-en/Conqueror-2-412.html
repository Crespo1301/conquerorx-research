1.6 Experiences

To add an experience press Add and select an Experience from the Experience list. The Opening Type and Graphic Set fields get automatically populated with the first available option in alphabetical order; to change it, use the drop-down menu. The same Experience can be added multiple times as long as it gets associated with a different Opening Type.

MMS Experiences are defined individually. The center can define as many experiences as they want and combine them with different times of the year or moments of the week. Each MMS experience is linked to a number, to prevent any experience from changing it when it gets associated with a different monitor.

Note: the same experience can be played on multiple monitors, e.g. experience 2 can be played on monitor "Entrance 2" and "VIP area 2".

The Price Subtitle and the second filed of the Tagline are not mandatory, but it could provide useful and relevant information such as the maximum number of players. The price shown on screen is the default price key of the Experience. When the default price key changes, the price on the MMS Experience changes accordingly in real time.

Each MMS Experience has his own "Graphic set", which defines the visual appearance in the monitors. Each Graphic set is made of a fixed graphic layout and background, and a predefined list of videos. Centers can add or remove videos from the list, according to the environments they are offering.

To preserve the integrity and quality of the feature, centers cannot add their own videos.
