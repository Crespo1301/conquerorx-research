3.3 Setting the Average Rules

Front Desk > Leagues > Setup > Handicap

Set how to establish the player average in the league.

Minimum Number of Games for Establishing League Average

Put in the number of games necessary to calculate the player average.

The player average used before playing this number of games is the Entering Average set for its category (in the table above) or specified for that player, in the Teams and Players window.

Apply League Average to Games Already Played

Enable this option if the average established after the number of games set above has to be retrospective. In this case, after establishing the average, a recalculation of points and handicaps of games played until that point is carried out.

Maintain the Same Average for the Entire Series

This option is always active: it means that the player's average is used from the beginning of the series to the end of it (the player's average is not recalculated while playing the series).
