4.1 How does it Work?

The cashier can be set to be able to use a Money Pouch and/or a hardware cash drawer.

Each cashier can have the Work with a Money Pouch privilege and/or Work with a Cash Tray privilege.

The cashier can have both privileges or only one. When the cashier with both privileges logs in the system, they will have to specify with which cash drawer they are going to work:

This window is shown only in the case of a real choice:

  • if both the hardware cash drawer are already busy, the cashier will be automatically connected to a Money Pouch.
  • if the cashier can work only on a hardware cash drawer and one is already busy, they will be automatically connected to the available cash drawer

In order to view, park, close and print a shift pertaining to another operator, the staff member has to have the Manage cash drawer sessions and shifts opened by others privilege enabled. They will see the Remove Drawer and the Join Shift buttons enabled:

  • Join Shift allows the operator with a cash drawer connected to that terminal working on the currently selected shift. When joining a shift pertaining to another operator, the new operator name in the upper bar is highlighted in order to point up this "irregular" situation.
  • The Remove Drawer button allows disconnecting the hardware cash drawer from the terminal, and the shift remains open.

The cashier with a hardware cash drawer working on a shift on different terminals has to have the Work on shifts through different terminals privilege. In this case, they can move from a terminal to another with their cash tray using the Remove Drawer and Join Shift functions.

Logging off from terminal

To be completely logged off from the terminal, the operator with a closed shift has to press Remove Drawer to disconnect their hardware cash drawer, allowing the next operator to insert their cash drawer.

Managing Shifts

With the Personal Cash Drawer management this window displays all the shifts in a single table.

Note that the Sector column remains in order to have the old shifts correctly displayed.

The total displayed for Closed Shifts corresponds to the Shift Total printed on reports. If the shift is open, the total is calculated and displayed in real time.

Select a Personal Cash Drawer to generate reports related to that operator shift.
