8.2 Open Choice Menus

Packages can be composed of generic items to be specified afterwards (during or after sales, even after payment). For example, a package can include 1 "soft drink" without specifying the particular soft drink (the choice might be between "coke", "soda", "sparkling water", "lemonade", etc.).

In order to create an open choice menu, select the department, click on the Menu tab and then press New .

To choose the items that are going to be part of the menu being created, click on the table or press the small calculator located straight above the table.

In this window, add the items to the menu by selecting them on the left-hand side of the window and then press the green assign arrow. Similarly, to remove a previously chosen item, select it and press the red remove arrow.

After choosing the items that make up the menu, press OK and select which item is to be proposed by default (Default Choice ).

Enable the Receive Payment without Specifying Package Items in Center Setup in order to allow payment without specifying each individual item in the package deal. So, when selling a generic soft drink with this option disabled, it should be necessary to specify the particular soft drink (e.g. "coke", "soda", "sparkling water", "lemonade", etc.).
