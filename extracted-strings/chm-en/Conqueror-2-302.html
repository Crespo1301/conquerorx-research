2.6 Poker

The Poker game is simulated on the monitors whereby the player must bowl a Hot Shot in order to be dealt a card and form a poker hand.

From the moment in which a player obtains five cards, they can choose the first card to exchange (from the lane keyboard) and this exchange continues from the sixth Hot Shot to the end of the game. There is one winner per lane/pair of lanes.
