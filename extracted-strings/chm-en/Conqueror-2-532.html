12.2 Settings

Setup > Price Setup > Lane Order Menus

A maximum of 9 different price key groups can be created, and each Center can manage them in the way that best suits its needs.

Press Add Group , enter the name of the group to be created, select a Picture for it and press OK.

Then Select the Price Keys that will available in that group: select a Department and move the relevant price keys to the right-hand side of the screen by clicking on it and pressing on the assign arrow. Price keys coming from a different Department can be chosen; to do this just select another Department and move the relevant price keys.

The Department structure displays only POS departments, because only items belonging to the POS can be ordered directly from the lanes.

Menu Mode

Choose one of the following Lane Order options:

Complete Menu - normal mode, for which the price list is utilized on the lanes.

Off - deactivates Lane Orders for all the lanes, for example when the time of bar or kitchen closure is getting near.

Blinking Bar - by pressing the Bar button on the lane keyboard, the upper section of the players' grid flashes and SOS is displayed in the corresponding lane icon in Lane Status advising the operator of the call (for those not utilizing the price list).

With Bowland it is possible to deactivate the Lane Orders and the relative Bar key on the lanes through the Lane Options.

Pictures

Prepare a set of pictures in the common formats png, bmp, jpg for categories and price keys; category pictures should be 284x224 pixels; price key pictures should be 284x172 pixels - If the pictures are smaller or bigger, they will be automatically adjusted to be displayed in the best way.

In the case of a price key or a category without any picture, name and price are displayed in the center of the related box.

Display Without Taxes Notice on Console

Select this option if prices contained in the current lane order menu don't include taxes.

If taxes are not included, a Without Taxes label is displayed near the total price, making customers aware that taxes are to be added.

Skip Amount Tendered

When customers finish making their bar orders they have to specify the amount they will tender, so that the operator can easily prepare the necessary change. Enable this option to skip this step.

Automatically Save in Tab and Print

Enable this option to directly send the order to the kitchen or to the bar skipping the operator review through the Urgent Tasks window.

In this case, a tab is automatically created upon lane ordering.

Customized Font

Click on the Font icon in order to customize the layout of lane order receipts.

To remove the customization and go back to the default, disable the Custom Font option.

Quick Button for Urgent Tasks

Setup > Terminal Setup > Quick Buttons

Here the Lane Orders Urgent Tasks Quick Button can be enabled.
