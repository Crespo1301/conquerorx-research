5. Squad Setup

Front Desk > Tournaments > Squads

This window is utilized in the management of squads. At the top of the screen the Tournament , Event and Squad fields reveal the name of the tournament and the event containing the relative squad.

This module contains four tabs: Global , Teams and Players , Movement and Point Assignment .

If there are not any squads in the selected event, they can be created by pressing the New button.
