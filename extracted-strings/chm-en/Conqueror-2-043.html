8. Refunds

POS > Refunds

The Refund module provided by Conqueror is a tool that allows the operator to perform and record all refund operations. Refunds may be necessary in various situations: if the item required is no longer available although it has already been paid for, or if the customer is for any reason not completely satisfied, or simply if the customer is to be refunded their deposit.

Click on the item to be refunded, modify the Price if necessary (e.g. if only part of the price or a larger amount is to be refunded). If desired, a reason for the refund can be entered in the Note space and it is also possible to choose to print this note in Receipts and/or in Shift Reports .

To assign the current refund to a member, press Search ; this also allows the refunding of Members Only items and discounted items.

The Undo button clears the table, while the Payment button allows moving directly to the Payment window to carry out usual transactions (Split, Link Tab, Receipt).
