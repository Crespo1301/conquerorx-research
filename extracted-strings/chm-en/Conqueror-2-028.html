2.1 Selling Items

First click on the category, then on the item to be sold: the item selected is displayed within the table in the left-hand area of the screen, indicating the name of the Product , its Quantity and its Total price.

As selections are made, the Total located straight under the items is automatically updated, displaying the total amount.

Selling Multiple Items

The system allows the sale of more than one item of the same kind in two quick and easy ways, giving the operator the opportunity to choose the most suitable one.

To sell more than one item of the same kind:

  • Click on the item twice to sell 2 items, click on it three times to sell 3 items, etc. In this case every click is a row in the list.
  • Press the desired numeric key, then select the item to be multiplied. If the quantity is greater than 7, click on ?x and enter the quantity of the item. In this case a single row is displayed in the list.

To modify the quantity relating to an item, click on the related row, press Change Quantity and enter the new quantity.

Adding Modifiers

Modifiers are displayed when a price key with Automatic modifiers is selected: click on the desired modifier and press OK .

To assign a modifier to a price key to which no automatic modifier has been assigned, press Modifiers , click on the desired modifier and then press OK .

The modifier is added by default. If a modifier has to be removed from a price key, press Without before pressing OK .

By default the modifier is Optional : i.e. during sales the operator has to press Modifiers to access the choice of the modifier. In this case the modifier window must be opened by the operator when selling the price key.

Selecting Menu Choices

If the package selected has an open choice menu, click on Choose Menu to specify the customer's choice. If for example, the package includes 1 generic soft drink, click here to specify the particular soft drink from coke, sparkling water, lemonade, etc.

An item (e.g. coke) could already be selected when opening the menu choices: this means it was previously set as Default Choice in the Price Setup, because it is the one chosen more frequently.

Only enabling the Receive Payment without Specifying Package Items option in Center Setup allows payment without specifying each individual item in the package deal.

Inserting a Note

Allows writing a note that can be displayed under the selected line. In the window that opens it is possible to specify whether to print it in receipt and/or in reports.

Deleting a Row

Select the bill row to be removed and press here to delete it (price key or note).

A price key cannot be deleted if it was previously saved in a tab, but it can be voided.

Voiding a Price Key

Voids the selling of the selected price key.

When voiding a package in the POS window, a single row is displayed instead of displaying a row for each package item in order to decrease the space that voided items occupy in the bill.

Voiding a transaction row related to the beverage dispenser is considered a Suspect Action and is therefore reported in the Shift Reports.

Applying Discounts

Manual discounts can be applied to an entire bill (including taxes) or to individual price keys, by selecting them before choosing the discount. Click on Discount , whereby the available discounts that can be applied within that particular time zone are displayed. Click on one of the discounts and press OK to apply that discount to the bill.

When a discount is applied manually to a member with a regular discount, the higher of the two discounts applies. Example: if an operator decides to apply a 20% discount to a family's total bill, the 20% discount will apply to all family members except the mother, whose 30% discount still applies as it is higher than the other discount.

When applying a manual discount in the Sales window, the chosen mode (Apply to Selected Items or Apply to Entire Bill ) is now set as default for the further manual discount applications on that terminal.

The relevant privilege is required to perform manual discounts with the Authorization Required option.

Clearing a Bill

Press Undo to clear the table.

Assigning a Transaction to a Member

A bill can be assigned to a specific member in order to:

  • record financial data relating to that specific member (print reports and allow the member to collect award points),
  • sell Members only items,
  • allow the member to pay with QCash,
  • give them their member discount
  • allow them to pay with their Member Account and print them an invoice etc.

To link a bill to a member, swipe the FB card or press Search : this opens a search window where members can be found by inserting their name or ID.

Adding a Tip

Click on Tips to open the Tip & Gratuity Management.

Creating a New Tab

The current bill can become a tab by simply clicking on Save : the tab creation window pops up and is automatically associated to the operator currently logged in, and by default is called "Terminal n - hh:mm"; enter a meaningful name (so it can be easily identified) and press Enter on the keyboard or press OK .

If the tab holder is a member, click on the Member field and select the FB.

To operate with a tab belonging to another operator, the Access to Tabs Pertaining to other Operators privilege is necessary.

Saving Items within a Tab

To save the current items within an existing tab, click on Add to Tab , and in the window that opens:

  • double click on the relevant tab

or

  • select it and press Choose .

The tab is automatically opened in the Sales window, completed with the newly added items (the items saved earlier are displayed in blue, while the new ones are in black).

Managing Tabs

Click on Tabs to directly access the complete Tab Management.

Proceeding with Payment

Payment opens the Payment window, which allows various financial operations: a bill can be paid, paid with receipt, split into two or more bills and different Payment Modes can be managed.

Undoing a Payment and Reprinting a Receipt

Click on Reprint to directly access the POS > Reprint Receipts module, where it is possible to:

  • choose and print a particular receipt,
  • undo a payment in order to change the payment mode after the receipt has been issued,
  • reopen a bill closed by mistake.
Only operators provided with the Reprint Receipts privilege have the possibility to proceed.
