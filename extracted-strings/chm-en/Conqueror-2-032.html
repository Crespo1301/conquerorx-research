3. Tabs

POS > Tabs

Enable the Use Tabs Screen as POS Main Window option in the POS Setup in order to display the Tab Management screen as main screen of the POS Module.

The tabs currently open are represented by the boxes in the central part of the window.

Only one click on the tab is necessary to display its details (description, operator, bill details). A second click opens it in the Sales window.

To operate on the tab, double click on it or select it and press Choose : the Sales window is accessed and it is possible to add, modify and delete items (the items saved before are displayed in blue, while the new ones are in black).

To operate with a tab belonging to another operator, the Access to Tabs Pertaining to other Operators privilege is necessary.

Creating a Tab Manually

A tab can be created manually:

  • in the Sales window after item selection by clicking on Save
  • in this window by pressing New : in this case the operator is automatically sent to the Sales window for the sales.

In both cases the tab is associated to the operator who is currently logged in and by default is called "Terminal n - hh:mm", but it is advisable to enter a meaningful name to the tab so it can be easily identified.

If the tab holder is a member, click on the Member field and select the FB.

By clicking on OK the Sales window where items can be assigned opens immediately.

Sorting Tabs

To make the tab search faster, use the Sorting by scroll down menu and choose from Description , Date-Ascending , Date-Descending : the tabs are automatically arranged according to the chosen criteria.

Works in both the open and closed tabs screen.

Deleting a Tab

To delete a tab, select it and press Delete .

Note that a tab can be deleted only if empty.

Tabs Automatically Created

Tabs are automatically created when opening a lane or when setting a reservation as Arrived (or Ready).

  • Lane tabs can be displayed by pressing Lanes on the right-hand side of the window: each lane is represented by a box that can be selected.
  • Reservation tabs can be displayed by pressing Booking on the right-hand side of the window: incoming reservations and current reservations are represented by boxes that can be selected.

From the POS workstation it is possible to sell items to a reservation without opening the Reservation Sheet or the Lane Control window: simply click on the Booking or Lanes button, select the desired reservation or lane and press Choose .

Incoming Reservation Tabs

Incoming Reservations can be displayed and managed.

To create a tab for an incoming reservation click on Next Bookings , select the desired incoming reservation and press Choose to add items directly to the tab.

If the incoming reservation is a Provisional or Confirmed reservation, when creating a tab for it the reservation is automatically set as Arrived and the tab is displayed in the Tab window.

Merging Tabs

To merge 2 or more tabs:

      1. open the first tab to be merged,
      2. press Add to Tab and select the second tab to be merged,
      3. press Choose and the POS items are automatically moved/added to the first tab.
      4. By pressing Save the inclusive tab is closed, with all the items grouped in it.

This procedure can be repeated on more tabs, even on tabs related to reservations or lanes, e.g. customer drinks or meals can be linked to their lane when they start to bowl.

To restore the original tabs, press Unmerge : the tab is automatically split in two separated tabs.

Credit Card and Seamless Transactions

Thanks to tab management it is possible to provide customers with a single bill for their entire visit including bowling, restaurant and other items purchased at the snack bar, on the lanes or in any area of the Bowling Center: the operator swipes the customer's credit card at the beginning of the evening and all customer purchases will be added to their tab. When they leave the Center the total tab amount will be automatically charged on the credit card previously swiped.

The name of the tab automatically includes the name of the card holder, the terminal number, the date and the time of tab opening.

All tabs linked to a specific credit card will automatically be merged in the Payment window.

Printing the Bill

Press Bill to print the bill to give to the customer before collecting the payment.

This bill is not the receipt. The receipt is printed after payment and it contains payment modes, change, taxes, discounts, etc.
