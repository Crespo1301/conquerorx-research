1. MMS Monitor Management

Setup > Technical Setup > Multi Media System

The first tab, Monitors , contains a scroll menu for each MMS monitor previously set in the Setup tab.

The colored dot indicates the status of the MMS: when functioning it is green and when it is inactive it is red.

Select the session or the sequence to be displayed from the scroll menu for each monitor.

Press Video Source and enter the video source address in order to use the television features.

When choosing a score, standings or Strike Challenge session, a supplementary option appears where the lane involved is specified.

On the monitors, the various sessions can be display by rotation. E.g. 10 seconds of advertising, 30 seconds of waiting list, 1 minute TV, 15 seconds of standings etc. Different Sequences have to be set, as a succession of Score sessions, Waiting List sessions, Advertising playlist, Standing sessions, TV sessions, Price List sessions.
