3.2 Closing Cash Drawer Sessions

To close a cash drawer session, press Close in the Shift module and print the cash drawer session report.

A cash drawer session can be parked by a cashier (with the appropriate privileges), permitting the operator to count the moneys received on another terminal that does not necessarily pertain to that cash drawer or sector.

When closing a cash drawer session, all other terminals connected to that Shift receive a message inviting them to log off in order to close the Shift and other users are not permitted to connect.

When all terminals have disconnected from the cash drawer session, the moneys received can be counted if set accordingly.

Once the moneys received have been counted, the End of Shift Reporting window opens and the Shift report can be printed and/or exported (automatically or manually) through QuCad and QuickBooks Desktop.
