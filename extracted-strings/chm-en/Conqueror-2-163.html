3.4 Moneys Received Summary

In the case the Count Float on Closure option has been enabled, the Moneys Received Summary window appears when closing a Shift/cash drawer session. This window functions similarly to the Sales window, whereby the operator selects the denomination and then specifies the amount of that particular denomination that is in the cash drawer.

If the Display Total in Money Summary option has been enabled, totals related to Special Payments are automatically calculated and displayed.

The Total is updated as the money is counted into the system. If typing errors are made, press Reset to empty the table. Press OK when all moneys have been accounted for in the system.

At this point a window reveals the Starting Float , the Net Cash Counted , the System Calculated Total and the money Over/Short of the calculated total.

To go back to the Shift window in order to correct any counting errors or the like, press No or press Yes to continue, where confirmation of the amount deposited for the next cash drawer session is required in the case the Float Confirmation option has been set. Press OK to confirm the proposed amount or insert a new amount.

If the Notes, Weather and Staff option is set, it is also necessary to confirm the involved operators. The operator can also enter the Weather present during the Shift and also insert a Note describing specific conditions present during the Shift.

Once the operation is complete, press OK to proceed to the End of Shift Reporting window.

The cashier inserts all data before seeing the report, thus avoiding the manipulation of income. If, however, the cashier requests to Preview the report without printing it, this is registered as a Suspect Action in the System Log.
