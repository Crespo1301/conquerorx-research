11. Payment Provider Setup

License and Additional Hardware

A specific Conqueror Pro license (and/or additional hardware) is required to use a payment provider.

Payment Mode Activation

After configuring the Center's provider, it is necessary to activate and personalize the related Payment Mode in the Denomination Setup: go to Setup > Price Setup > Denominations, click in the Special Payments column, press New , enter a Description and select the relevant Payment Type (Credit card, Debit card, etc.)

Provider Setup

Setup > Center Setup > Payments

These settings are set during the installation of the system.

It is not recommended that operators modify any of these settings without first having received instructions from a qualified technician of QubicaAMF Technical Support.

Enter the Local Setup and the Web Setup to specify the provider settings.
