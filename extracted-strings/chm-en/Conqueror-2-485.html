9.1 High Score Standings

There are four predefined High Scores: Daily, Weekly, Monthly and All-Time. The start and end time are based on the "business day"; therefore, some High Scores may include games played after midnight up to the date change time defined in Conqueror Pro. The week starts on the day selected as "First day of the week" in Center Setup, the month starts from day 1, and the start date of the All-Time High Scores coincides, by default, with the first opening of the setup.
If a more recent start date is set for the All-Time High Scores; games, older than the new date, will be deleted according to the rules defined in Conqueror Pro.
The center can define up to five custom High Scores to cover special times of the year (Christmas, Easter, Halloween, Spring Break and so on) or special events arranged by the center. An active custom High Score is displayed and celebrated on its specific date range only. E.g.: Christmas High Scores will be displayed and celebrated only at Christmas. Note: two custom High Scores cannot run at the same time.
If two players have the same score, the system follows these criteria to determine their position in the ranking:
· the bowler that played fewer levels gets a better position in the standings
· the bowler that played the session before the others gets a better position in the standings
The session score, used to enter the rankings, is displayed on SuperTouch in the Session Progress and Totals screens.
