1. Overview

The management of the lanes is contained for the most part in the Lane Status and Lane Control modules.

Lane functioning is regulated by the settings in Center Setup, the Lane Options and the Bowling Modes.

Bowling Center Options e.g. the Opening Modes in Center Setup, regulate the functioning of the entire Center and are controlled by the Bowling Center management staff, due to their importance. Many default options can also be set and modified when necessary, making this process quick and easy.

Dynamic Price Keys can be set by default, as well as Lane Options (with standard configuration for certain situations such as Saturdays, children and glow bowling), Bowling Modes (a comprehensive set of Opening Modes, Price Keys and Lane Options that set everything up according to the Time Zones) and Bowler Options (individual player options). The more default values are set, the quicker the process of opening and closing the lane is.
