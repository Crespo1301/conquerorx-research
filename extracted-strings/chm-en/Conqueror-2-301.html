2.5 Rocky Road Race

The special ability Rocky Road Race game is preset to be played on a certain number of lanes.

The Rocky Road Race is a competition between all of the lanes, each lane with its own car that advances in the race as the players achieve Hot Shots.

After each Hot Shot is achieved on the lanes, a summary of the race placing is displayed on the monitors, highlighting the car in the lead.

The first car to cross the finish line by achieving the set number of Hot Shots, wins the race and a new race can begin.

There is also the possibility of continuing the game after the first car crosses the finishing line, until all prizes have been won through the Loop Mode setting.
