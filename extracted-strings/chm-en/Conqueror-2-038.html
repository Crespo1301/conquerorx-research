5.5 Reprinting a Receipt

POS > Reprint Receipts

First select the correct Date and Terminal followed by the desired transaction: the original transaction is immediately displayed on the left-hand side of the window, while the payment mode details are shown in the right-hand corner.

At this point, press Receipt to reprint the receipt currently displayed.

Only operators provided with the Reprint Receipts privilege have the possibility to proceed.
