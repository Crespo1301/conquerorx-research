10.4 Removing an Item

To delete an item (for which no payment has been taken) from the waiting list, select the item in the table and press Remove . If bowling sessions have been paid for regarding the item to be removed, the Remove button in the table is changed to Refund . Pressing the Refund button opens the Payment window complete with the details of the refund to be given. Once the refund has been performed, the item is automatically cancelled. If, when creating the item, Fast Sale Items or POS articles were bought that have not yet been paid for, the button changes to Pay & Delete and pressing it opens the Payment window ready to accept payment for these articles. Once payment has been received, the item is automatically cancelled.
