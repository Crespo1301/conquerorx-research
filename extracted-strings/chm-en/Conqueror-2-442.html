4.1 Best Scores Setup

There are several type of Best Scores standings that the system can display.

The center can enable only some of them, or disable all the standings. By default, they are all enabled.

The enabled best scores are shown on MMS when lanes are closed. The same best scores are also shown on SuperTouch when one player enters in the best scores, to celebrate the event.

The standings are kept distinct for "Bowling" games and the four "Skill Games". If a center doesn't use Skill Games, it is possible to uncheck the corresponding check box.

Note: Mad Games are not included in any standing.

There are four ranking types:

· Lane Today

Best scores played on this lane - today. This standing is reset each day.

· Lane History

Best scores played on this lane - all times.

· Center Today

· Best scores played in the center, on any lane - today. This standing is reset each day.

· Center History

Best scores played in the center, on any lane - all times.
