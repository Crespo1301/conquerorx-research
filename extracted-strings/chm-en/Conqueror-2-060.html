2. Key Concepts

2.1 Opening Modes

Payment is based on either the number of games (or frames) played, the time players spend playing, on unlimited time/games.

Games

When using this opening mode, the players are sold a certain number of games. Payments can also be based on number of frames; whereby payment is usually linked to dynamic price keys (that vary according to the time/day), even if the dynamic price keys can be used in any opening mode.

When charging per frame, payment is often associated with proportional payment, a method of precise calculation, set in Center Setup.

Time

The bowler is charged according to the time spent on the lane, whereby payment for the session is based on time intervals and (as with other modes) the price may vary during the course of the session, depending on the prices set for each time zone.

Unlimited

This opening mode can be applied either to lanes or players, depending on how settings are set in Center Setup. Tickets are sold to individual players or to be used on the lane, regardless of the number of players, allowing bowlers to play as long as they have paid for (not advisable for busy periods).

Coin-op

A coin-operated machine.
