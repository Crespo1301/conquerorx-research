9.4 Tax-exempt Exports

If an export to Micros, R-Keeper or WinTic is configured, the add-on tax exemption does not modify the behavior of the system, because tax information is not actually exported.

No information related to tax exemption is available on exports to third party programs as RecTrac or Zonal.

Tax exemption is disabled when CleanCash export is enabled.

Export to Dassle (QCAD)

The operator can configure the tax-exempt suffix for Dassle. That suffix is appended to price key information during export to Dassle when the price key is sold with tax-exemption.

Export to QuickBooks Desktop

The operator can configure the tax-exempt suffix for QuickBooks Desktop. That suffix is appended to price key information during export to QuickBooks Desktop when the price key is sold with tax-exemption and the system is configured to export to QuickBooks Desktop without using account numbers.

In QuickBooks Desktop Setup, the operator can choose to define a second distinct account number for each price key. That second account number is used for price keys sold with tax exemption, when the system is configured to export the information to QuickBooks Desktop using account numbers.
