1.1 Waiting List

The Waiting List can be displayed on the MMS screens: in the Monitors tab, choose Waiting List in the relevant MMS scroll menu.

Click on Waiting List to enter the message to be displayed on the right-hand side of the screen of the MMS screen. By default, the Go to Front Desk message is entered, but it can be overwritten.

To have the PIP TV in the Waiting List, choose the Waiting List + PIP TV in the scroll down menu.

MULTI MEDIA SYSTEM > MMS Monitor Management > Waiting List
