15.10 Signature Grids

BES Only
Setup > Bowling Setup > Lane Options > Basic > Environment

Click on the picture preview to choose the Environment to be displayed on the lane monitors. In the window that opens, simply click on the environment picture to send it to the lane.

By clicking on Custom , the Signature Grids is accessed, where it is possible to choose its style, colors and a mix of animation themes to be displayed.

When setting a Signature Grid , the Environment from Console option is automatically disabled.

Foreground and Background

In the window that opens first choose a Foreground between those available and then choose a Background .

Change Grid Color with Game

Enable the Change Grid Color with Game option to automatically change the grid color at each game (Game 1 is green, game 2 yellow, 3 red, 4 green and so on). In this case Background options are disabled.

Loading Custom Pictures

In addition to those available (fixed in the system), it is possible to load a picture by clicking on + .

Pictures loaded in this module are kept in Conqueror Pro until they are deleted with the eraser button.

After loading 150 pictures, it is necessary to delete some of them in order to be able to load others.

All common file formats are supported (jpg, png, bmp, gif). If the pictures are smaller, they will be centered on the screen, while if the pictures are bigger they will be downsized.

Animation Set

At last choose one or more Animation Sets between those available in the list on the right-hand side and click on OK to save your custom grid.

Webcam Picture

It is also possible to take a photo with the webcam and use it as grid background. Press on the photo camera icon and take the picture. If the picture is ok to be used, press Save . If not, press again on Take Photo .

Picture Validation

Front Desk > Lane Control > Picture Validation

Pictures taken by the webcam can be validated by the operator through the Picture Validation module, accessible also through a Quick Button.

If the picture validation has been set in the Lane Options, the Quick Button lights on when there are pictures to be validated.

Click on the picture to be removed from the lanes and press Apply .

If other pictures to be validated are present, they are automatically loaded after pressing Apply .
