3. Managing a Trouble Call

3.1 Dealing with Trouble Calls through Lane Status

Front Desk > All Lanes

To acknowledge, cancel or complete a Trouble Call from the Lane Status module, click on the relevant lane icon and press Mechanic Service whereby a panel appears allowing the operator to Acknowledge , Complete or Cancel the Trouble Call.

The icons in the Lane Status module reflect the status of the Trouble Call in real time, allowing the operator to be up to date with the Trouble Calls and when it is possible to re-open lanes.

These actions can also be performed by the mechanic, using the telephone.
