2.4 Christmas

The Christmas Special Game is a game of luck accompanied by a winter, snowy environment.

After the Pre-start Games, the Special Game appears on the monitors of all lanes set to play, one of which is chosen randomly and the customer bowling on that lane at the time, either wins or loses the game (depending on the frequency of the winning games and the total number of prizes to be won per session).

When playing the Christmas Special Game, the Father Christmas figure appears on the monitor with his sack full of presents, he extracts a present which is usually small and silly if the sequence is lost (e.g. a plastic fish or a carrot). If on a winning sequence, the present extracted would be something of an expensive nature such as a diamond.
