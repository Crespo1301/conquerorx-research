32. Game Printouts

Front Desk > Lane Control > Game Printouts

All score data related to games can be filtered, displayed and printed (for bowlers who want a written copy of the scores achieved), through the Game Printouts screen.

It is possible to choose the type of printout according to the standing and the score, and select additional details to display in the printouts.

This module can also be accessed through Lane Status and Lane Control by pressing Print Games .

The data printed in the report header are the official Bowling Center's name and address entered upon activation of the Conqueror license.
