12.4 Promotions

In this tab it is possible to define limited-time promotions that allow members belonging to a particular category to earn more points than usual.

To create a new promotion:

  • press New ,
  • specify a name for the promotion,
  • select when the promotion will be effective (from...to...)
  • digit a Multiplier for the points collected in the period specified above.

In this way the member belonging to the specified category included in the date range will have their collected points multiplied by the specified Multiplier .
