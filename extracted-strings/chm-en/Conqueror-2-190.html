6.1 Managing Receipts and Invoices

Back Office > FBT > Invoice

In this module the receipts put in member accounts are displayed and grouped by members; to visualize only the receipts pertaining to a specific member, press Search and select the member in the database.

Select one or more receipts and press New Invoice to create the invoice to be sent to the member in order to seek payment: specify the Invoice ID (compulsory data in order to proceed) and a Due Date for payment. By pressing OK the Invoices tab automatically opens and it is possible to:

  • load a header picture and a footer text before printing the invoice (Page Setup )
  • preview and print the invoice
  • delete/void the invoice
  • visualize all the invoices that have to be paid (All invoices )
  • confirm the invoice payment
The VAT Number automatically printed on the invoice is the one previously specified in the member data.

When receiving the payment of an invoice, search for it by pressing Search , enter the Invoice ID and then proceed with the registration of the payment in the Payment window.

When voiding an invoice, the receipts previously selected are replaced in the Pending Receipts and will be available to be selected again to create a new invoice.
