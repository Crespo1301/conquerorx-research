15.11 Selfie Grids

BES Only
Setup > Bowling Setup > Lane Options > Basic > Environment

It allows bowlers to select a foreground from those available and set a selfie as background image. Click on the Selfie Grid button to open the Select Animations for Selfie Grids dialog window; and use is to add/remove animations.

When setting a Selfie Grid , the Environment from Console option is automatically disabled.

Foreground and Background

Players can choose a Foreground from those available and then take a selfie and use it as Background .

Picture Validation

Front Desk > Lane Control > Picture Validation

Pictures taken by the webcam can be validated by the operator through the Picture Validation module, accessible through a Quick Button. If the picture validation has been set in the Lane Options, the Quick Button flashes when there are pictures to be validated. Click on the picture to be removed from the lanes and press Apply . If there are other pictures to be validated, these automatically load after pressing Apply .
