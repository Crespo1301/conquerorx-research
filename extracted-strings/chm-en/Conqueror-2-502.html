3.3 Managing Tax Groups

To create a tax group, press New (on the left-hand side of the screen).

Then, define the name of the group (the system proposes a default name that can be changed) and use the arrows in the middle of the screen to assign a tax to the group or remove one from it.

Pressing Delete cancels the selected group of taxes.

Once again, make any necessary changes and press Save .
