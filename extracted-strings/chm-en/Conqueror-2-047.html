9.3 Reporting

Summary Section in Shift Report

Staff operators can choose to add to the shift report a section that shows the details of receipts marked as Tax-exempt, similarly to refund and void sections.

Shift Report Grouped by Tax Exemption

When printing a shift report, a staff operator can choose to group the price keys sold with tax exemption or without tax exemption.

Historical Report Grouped by Tax Exemption

When printing an historical report, a staff operator can choose to group price keys sold with tax-exemption or without tax-exemption.
