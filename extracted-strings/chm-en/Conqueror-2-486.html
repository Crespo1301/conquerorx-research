9.2 Standings

At the end of each Hyper games, the system verifies whether there are events to celebrate and displays celebratory animations on overhead monitors. A bowler is celebrated either when they enter the standings or raise in ranking. Players gets celebrated for two achievements at most:

· Best result obtained in a predefined ranking
· Any position obtained in a custom ranking
If no custom High Scores has been defined, and the center has only two predefined standings; the bowler is celebrated for one predefined High Score only.
The player, entering multiple predefined standings, is celebrated only for one, according to the following prioritization of events:
· Ranks first in one of them
· Rises in ranking (also including the case in which the player enters the standings)
If the same event occurs (e.g. the bowler ranks first in the Daily and All-Time High Scores), the player is celebrated for the most prestigious High Score only: All-Time (most prestigious) while Daily (less prestigious).
During on-screen celebrations, players can change their names to amend any typos. To avoid idle time, the rename screen has a timeout system at the end of which the window closes automatically.
