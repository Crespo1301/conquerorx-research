7.2 Viewing Prizes

BES Only

When closing lanes, the View Prizes button is activated in the Lane Control and in the Score windows. By clicking on it the names of the customers who have won prizes are displayed and a receipt can be printed:

  • for all players who have won prizes

or

  • for one specific winner (by using the scroll menu).
This option is activated upon lane closure if prizes have been won on that lane.

LANE MANAGEMENT > Closing a Lane > Viewing Prizes
