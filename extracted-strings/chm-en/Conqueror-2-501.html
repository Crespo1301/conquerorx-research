3.2 Managing Taxes

To create a tax, press New (on the right-hand side of the screen).

The Delete button cancels the tax selected. If the tax belongs to a group, the system will not allow the cancellation.

With the four arrows in the lower right section of the screen the user can scroll through the predefined taxes, while the two arrows in the middle of the screen allow the assignment of a tax to a group, or its removal.

Make the necessary changes and press Save .

The following is a brief description of the settings:

Tax

Enter the Name of the tax.

Percentage/Fixed Value

The selection of one of these excludes the other, indicating whether the tax is to be calculated as a percentage or as a specified value.

From/To Value

Enter the range of monetary values to which the tax is to be applied (e.g. tax brackets could change, in relation to different amounts).

RecTrac Code

Enter the tax code for RecTrac.

Rounding Precision/Rounding Type

The Rounding Precision relates to the value to which the amount is to be rounded, while Rounding Type allows the operator to define whether amounts are to be rounded down, up or to the nearest value.

For example, if the amount to be paid is $5.02 dollars and is to be rounded up to the nearest 10 cents, the final amount will be $5.10. If it is to be rounded down to the nearest 10 cents, the final amount will be $5.00.

Level

It is possible to define more tax application levels: level 1 taxes are applied on the net total, level 2 taxes on the total including level 1 taxes and so on.

Enter 1 in the Level field to define the first tax to be applied, then enter 2 to define the second tax level, etc.

Included in Price

This setting indicates whether the tax should be included in the price or added at moment of payment.

Applied to Single Item

Specify whether the tax should be applied to each item sold on the same row of the transaction.

Apply to the Total of Item Value of Same Tax Group

For Canadian Market

As requested by the Canadian market, this tax calculation rule allows applying the tax percentage depending on the amount of all the price keys of the same tax category: instead of being applied to the single price keys, taxes are applied to the total of the price keys belonging to the same tax group.

Enable this option in order to use this tax calculation rule.
