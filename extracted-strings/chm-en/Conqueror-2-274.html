4. Standings

Front Desk > Swedish League > Standings

This module allows the league results to be filtered and then exported, printed, sent to the lane monitors as a scrolling message, or previewed.

To produce a classification, select the Type in the upper section of the window.

The report displays a section for each team, containing the player list with the total number of pins knocked down in the four series played, the general total of pins and the number of games played; under which there is a summary of the points earned by the team in each series and the general total.

For the Elite Series classification there is a section for each game, containing a row for each lane, with the corresponding players, the totals achieved and the bonuses won. The totals of both teams playing the game are present as well as the overall total for the two teams at the base of the panel.

The other settings are similar to those of the tournaments, just check the All column or select the league name to filter the data.
