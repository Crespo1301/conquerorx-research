2.1 Settings

Setup > Price Setup > Currencies

Here follows a brief description of this screen:

  • Type indicating which currency is set as the Main , Secondary or Other currency that the Center may use.
  • ID is the international coding of the currency (e.g. USD).
  • The Name of the currency (e.g. Dollar).
  • Conversion referring to the exchange rate of that currency, in relation to the main currency.
