9. Group Lane Management

Good management of groups of lanes is useful when actions need to be performed very quickly. Opening modes are standard so the player names don't have to be inserted and the Fast Sale/POS items are assigned to the lane. Therefore the procedure is a lot faster, with less precise settings. The operations and settings applied during lane opening to the lanes selected, can be personalized by modifying the individual lane. To modify an individual lane, select the appropriate lane in the Lane Status window and go through the modification process of inserting player names, lane options, etc. This feature is also useful when applying Special Functions to more than one lane.
