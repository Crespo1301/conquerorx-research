9.2 Discount Setup

Setup > Price Setup > Discounts

To create a new type of discount, press New , enter the Name (e.g. school children) and set the following options:

Rate/Fixed Amount

Define the discount Rate or Fixed Amount .

In both cases different discount rates can be applied to different days/times in the dynamic table.

If the tab is not linked to a reservation, to a lane or to a time game, the discount can be inserted into the tab before payment time and then be saved in the tab.

It is also possible to define a 100% discount, allowing to "sell" single items or an entire bill for free.

When creating a price key, insert the Price that has to be assigned to it.

Clicking in the relevant field, the Define Dynamic Value for Discount window opens containing a table in which it is possible to specify whether the price key is to have the same price every day and at any time of the day, or different prices at different times or days, thus creating a Dynamic price key.

If the same price is to be applied to the whole table, insert the Price and press Apply or Enter on the keyboard.

In order to assign the price to a specific time zone, the whole table must be deselected by clicking on All . Then, select the area of the table (day of the week or specific time by clicking at the beginning of a row or at the top of a column) to which the price has to be to applied and press Apply . If the item is not to be sold in a particular time zone, select it and press Deactivate and the area concerned is cleared.

To select an interval of time, press the Shift key on the keyboard and click on the first and the last cells of the desired area. If single cells of the table are to be selected, press the Ctrl key on the keyboard and click on the cells to be selected.

To scroll through the timetable displaying the three different time zones of the bowling day (divided in 30 minute cells) use the two arrows on the right. Here the earliest time displayed is the one set as Day Changing Time.

The last three columns of the table, called EX1, EX2, EX3, represent three extra holidays that each Center can define for its particular needs, by going to Setup > Calendar Setup.

Authorization Required

This option limits the use of the manual discount only to operators with the Apply Authorization Required Discounts privilege.

Apply Discount also to Other Bowlers

Option available only for percentage discounts

This option extends the discount of a member to all their fellow bowlers.

Therefore, if a member belongs to a category with a discount containing this option checked, their discount will be applied to the whole bill (e.g. all family members or friends, etc.) and not to the individual customer alone (except in cases where the individual customer already has the right to a higher discount).

For example, if the father had a discount of 20% with this option checked, the whole family would benefit from the 20% discount except the mother, because she has a 30% discount.

Member Category Only

Option available only for percentage discounts

Set the Member Category Only option to send the current discount to the FBT Setup, where the discount can be linked to one or more Member Categories.

Note that this discount cannot be applied manually in the Payment window; it can only be associated to a category of members and be applied automatically.

External Key

If working with a third party program, set the relevant External Key .

Italian and Romanian Market

Fixed discounts on single price keys are managed for both the Italian and the Romanian markets.

For a technical limit of WinTic on rounding and subdivisions of the account part of SIAE, in the Italian market it is necessary to avoid using percentage discount on the single price keys.

For a technical limit of WinTic on rounding, it is necessary to avoid using discounts on the transaction total (for both markets).
