2.2 Recharging Prepaid Cards

CoinTech, Embed, Intercard, Sacoa

The prepaid card button in the POS main window opens a little window where it is possible to activate cards and to recharge them.

To activate a new card, simply swipe it and press Activation - Note that Sacoa cards are not activated by Conqueror .

To recharge the prepaid card, swipe it, enter the recharge amount and press Recharge : the recharge is automatically added to the current open tab.

One or more recharges can be added to the tab, allowing the customer to pay for them together with the other price keys in a single bill, e.g. if a family wants to recharge 2 cards, it is possible to pay for them together with a credit card.

In the Shift Reports recharge payments are listed in the Trust (Deposit) department.

To check if the card is active and the amount available on it, simply swipe it in the POS or payment window.
