6.2 Settings

Enabling the FB's Member Account

Front Desk > Frequent Bowlers > Account

By setting a Debt Limit different than zero the member automatically has the possibility to use the Member Account feature.

Their Account Balance is displayed and constantly updated: after a receipt is issued to that member, their debt to the center grows to pre-set limit.

Setting the Member Account Payment Mode

Setup > Price Setup > Denominations

To enter the Member Account payment mode, click in the Special Payments column, press New and enter the payment mode Description (that is a free choice) and select the related Payment Type , in this case choose Member Account .

Specifying the FB's VAT Number

Enter the VAT Number that has to be printed on the member's invoices under Front Desk > Frequent Bowlers > Advanced.

Setting the Invoice Page

Back Office > FBT > Invoice > Page Setup

The space available for the header picture is:

  • h: 8 cm (~ 3.15 in)
  • w: 9 cm (~ 3.54 in)
  • w/h = 1.125

If the dimensions are different the image is scaled, so the w/h ratio is important.

The maximum space available for the .rtf file is:

  • h: 3 cm (~ 1.18 in)
  • w: 19 cm (~ 7.48 in).
