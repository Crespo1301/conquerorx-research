9. Tax Exemption Handling

In the payment window the operator can to mark an entire transaction as Tax-exempt by specifying a Tax-exempt ID in the relevant field.

When a transaction is marked as Tax-exempt, all add-on taxes are removed and the balance is adjusted. The receipt shows the detail of Tax-exempt ID instead of the amount of add-on taxes.

It is possible to refund, undo and reprint a receipt with tax exemption.

Only the operators with the relevant privilege can perform tax exemption transactions. Employees without this new privilege are not able to insert a Tax-exempt ID in the payment window and in the FB data.
