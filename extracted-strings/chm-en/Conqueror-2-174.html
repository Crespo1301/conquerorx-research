9.1 Sector Setup

Setup > Center Setup > Sectors and Cash Drawers

In this window sectors and their cash drawers can be created.

On the left side of the screen, create a list of all the different sectors the Bowling Center is divided into (bowling, bar and so on), while on the right create the associated cash drawers with the corresponding float.

It is possible to create, Modify or Cancel sectors and cash drawers separately.

After following the steps to create sectors and cash drawers, set the right sector and cash drawer for each terminal through Terminal Setup > Preferences.

Exclusive Responsibility of Cash Drawers

A user privilege allows to define which operators can login on terminals already in use, joining the cash turn session of another operator; operators without this privilege can access only terminals not already associated to another operator, and cannot share the responsibility of their cash drawer with a colleague.

  • Enable the Exclusive Cash Drawer option to give the exclusive responsibility to use a particular cash drawer to the first operator who logs in and opens the cash turn session. The protected cash drawer will be accessed only by the operator who has opened the cash turn session and by any operator with the user privilege.
  • Give the Login Cash Drawer Session Opened by Another Operator user privilege to operators who can access all the cash drawers, including the ones pertaining to another operator. An operator without the privilege can access only their cash drawer.
