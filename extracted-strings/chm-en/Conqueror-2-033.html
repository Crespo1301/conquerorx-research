4. Gratuities and Tips

Usually the waiter takes the bill to the customer, who gives their credit card to the waiter. The waiter swipes the credit card and gives the customer their receipt. At this point, the customer writes the amount of the tip on the receipt. The waiter goes back to the cash register and inserts the amount of the tip in the Tips plugin.

Credit card payments and all the data related to them (ID , Date , Amount , Tip , Total , Tip to ) are displayed in the table. Select the bill to which the tip has to be associated, insert the Tip Amount and if desired assign it to a different operator (if not currently logged in) by using the Tip scroll down menu.

The Apply button makes the payment effective and the connection of the credit card transaction starts.

By default the table displays payments already made by credit card for which no tip has been given. To see transactions for which a tip has already been given, enable the Display all Transactions option.

Details relating to the transaction selected in the table are found in the box at the bottom of the window.

Only operators provided with the Manage Tips privilege have the possibility to proceed.

In the Sales window, enter a tip and print a receipt for the customer, displaying the bill amount, the tip applied, and the transaction total.

Automatic Gratuities

It is possible to pre-define which prices can be subject to a gratuity, which ones always require a gratuity and which are never to be considered (e.g. excluding alcoholic drinks where not allowed).

First define a default gratuity percentage (which can be optionally modified or amended at payment time).

The gratuity is automatically calculated when the bill includes at least a price key set as Always Include in Gratuity. The gratuity calculation is based on all the price keys in the bill set as Always Consider or Optionally Consider . The price keys set as Never Consider are not included in the gratuity calculation.

Collecting a Payment

From the payment window, press Tips to add a gratuity to the bill.

The gratuity is calculated on the basis of the percentage previously defined in Center Setup but can be manually modified by operators having the relevant user privilege.

The collected gratuity is not saved in the tab but is assigned to the waiter associated to the bill, if present, otherwise it is assigned to the operator currently logged in. In the case of a credit card payment, the gratuity is managed as a credit card tip.

In the Tips module it is possible to change a collected gratuity, in particular it is possible to modify the tip receiver in any case and the amount in the case of the following payment methods: credit cards, CoinTech and all the undefined payment methods (e.g. cash).

Only operators with the Change Automatic Gratuity Percentage can modify the gratuity percentage in the payment window.

Shift Reports

Gratuities and tips are properly tracked, displayed in a dedicated department in the Trust section of the End of Shift Reports and exported to QuickBooks Desktop.

Any transaction involving gratuities and tips is included in a receipt as for a normal trust price key and therefore it is displayed in a dedicated department in the shift report section displaying the trust items. So gratuity collection, additional tip collection and tip payouts are displayed in the shift report and included when calculating the amount present in the cash drawer. These transactions are also included in the other economic reports: Historical, Statistical and Frequent Bowler reports.
