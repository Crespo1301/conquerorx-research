6.1 Quick Squad Sign-in

From the Standings module it is also possible to take the first teams/players of a given standing and quickly sign them into a particular squad, selected manually from the squads still to be played. This allows to quickly sign in the first/best teams/players to an event without wasting any time entering all the names.

Create a Best Team tournament standing and press Sign in : the teams selected can automatically be enrolled in a squad in the same tournament for which the standing was created.

There is a similar feature for players, but the destination squad must contain teams made of at least 1 player.

This option works only for single players tournaments (no teams). Through this option, it is possible to sign in straight away, skipping the plug in team and players stage.

TOURNAMENTS > Standing Setup > Quick Squad Sign-in
