5.3 Paying Separately

A tab can be split into two or more different tabs, allowing the customer to pay at different stages, or allowing different customers to pay part of the whole amount (e.g. if members of a group decide to have separate bills).

To split a tab, press Split that opens a screen where the items to be split can be selected and moved from the table on the left to the one on the right, using the arrows in the middle.

After confirming the choice by pressing OK , the Payment screen opens, allowing the customer to pay their part of the total amount.

After the payment has been received, the splitting screen reopens and the tab can be split again.

Press Exit when split operations are completed, or press Save to save the remaining items in the tab.
