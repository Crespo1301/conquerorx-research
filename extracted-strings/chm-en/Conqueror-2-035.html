5.2 Special Payments

This module allows extremely flexible management of any of the payment modes a Bowling Center might accept, according to what has been set previously in the Denominations module.

It is possible to combine various payment modes and to collect money from different customers if desired, allowing customers the freedom to choose the payment solution that best fits their needs. A customer may choose to pay part of the payment due using QCash, part by Credit Card, and part Cash, etc.

Note that for Player Club Cash payments and for Member Accounts it is not possible to combine different payment types.

On the right-hand side of the screen there are boxes representing the different payment modes previously set (QCash, Credit Card, Check, Voucher, Prepaid Cards, etc.), while the small table contains the description and the amount of the different Special Payments.

Simply click on the desired Special Payment , set the amount and click on OK .

Paying with QCash

Select the QCash box, which opens the Member Search window: swipe the member's card or search and select the member in the archive.

The QCash Payment window opens, displaying data relating to the member previously selected. This data is set through Front desk > Frequent Bowlers and cannot be modified from this window.

On the right-hand side of the screen the amount to be paid with QCash can be defined (by default, the total amount due is suggested).

Search allows the operator to change the member through the Member Search window, while OK closes the window, calculating the Special Payment and the Total Due .

Paying by Credit Card

Swipe the credit card, confirm or edit the Amount to be paid and press OK .

Selecting the credit card box opens the small Credit Card window, where the Amount to be paid by credit card can be entered.

The two fields Masked Card Number and Expiry Date are automatically completed with the relevant information cannot be completed manually.

Paying by Debit Card

Swipe the debit card, confirm or edit the Amount to be paid and press OK .

A message reminds the customer to enter the PIN code using the PinPad device.

If swiping the credit card without first choosing the Debit Card Special Payment, the credit card payment is selected by default, but can be changed into a Debit Card payment by clicking on the To Debit Card button.

Paying with Player Club Cash

Select the Player Club Cash box, which opens the Member Search window: swipe the member's card or search and select the member in the archive. Selecting the member automatically completes the Payment Mode table with the relevant information.

Note that for Player Club Cash payments it is not possible to combine different payment types.

Paying with a Prepaid Credit Card

Swipe the Prepaid Card, confirm or amend the Amount to be paid and press OK .

Mixed payments are allowed, i.e. it is possible to pay part of the bill with the prepaid card and the rest with credit card, cash, free coupons, etc.

Partial Approval and Real Time Reversal - For Mercury, PCCharge and PAYwarePC, 'partial approval' and 'real time reversal' features are supported. In practice: if on the prepaid credit card the credit is not sufficient to pay the total amount due, the transaction is successfully processed and the remaining total due must be paid with another Payment Method. Otherwise, the transaction can be immediately voided.

Paying with Member Account

Select the Member Account box, which opens the Member Search window: swipe the member's card or search and select the member in the archive. Selecting the member automatically completes the Payment Mode table with the relevant information.

Press Pay without paying to add the receipt to the member Account: the receipt is immediately displayed in the Invoices module, where it can be managed.

Note that in Member Account it is not possible to combine different payment types.
