3.2 Teams and Players

Once the lanes have been set for the league created in the Global tab, the two teams (Home and Visitor) are automatically created in the Teams and Players tab with the default names entered in the Danish League tab in Center Setup for each team and player.

The list on the left contains the teams and clicking on a team reveals the players pertaining to that team, in the list on the right.

The Modify Team panel is identical to that of the tournaments. The Modify Player panel is different however, in that the Division section is deactivated as it is not used for the Danish Leagues and the order of the players cannot be modified as the list is non-existent.
