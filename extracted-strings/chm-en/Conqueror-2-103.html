13.2 Lucky Train

BES Only
Lane Status > Special Functions

A bell rings: Lucky Train crosses all lane monitors and then stops, leaving a car in front of each lane. All the car doors open and the player on the lane where Qupee steps down from the car wins, while arrows on the other lane monitors point to the winner's lane.

The Access to Lucky Train Special Function privilege is necessary to activate this game.

Lucky Train Setup

Press Lucky Train in the Special Functions to access the settings.

Select a group of Lanes on which the Lucky Train global game will be executed and choose a Winner (a random player is proposed but it can be changed with another one).

Set the Duration of the Lucky Train trip, i.e. the number of seconds between the moment it starts and the revelation of the winner. The suggested Range is calculated on the basis of the number of lanes and the number of players.

The suggested Volume of the Lucky Train game is by default the one applied to the lanes, but it can be modified.

Once the winner, the duration and the volume have been set, press Start to start the Lucky Train on the lanes.
