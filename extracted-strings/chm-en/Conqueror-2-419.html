1. Overview

Conqueror Pro solution specifically designed for the needs of bowling chains worldwide: Conqueror Pro's Call Center module provides bowling chains with a fully-functional call center for reservation services where a single Call Center can manage bookings for all centers in the bowling center chain.

A fast center-selection window allows operators to connect quickly to remote bowling centers and move rapidly between different centers. When connecting to a center the system has been specifically designed to speed up the whole booking process for Call Center operators by letting them define the key reservation details (date, time, number of bowlers) on the opening page.

The Call Center interfaces in real-time with the QubicaAMF Conqueror systems and servers in each remote center for seamless integration without the need for any manual intervention at the remote centers apart from the fundamental requirement that the Conqueror server at each center is accessible from the central Conqueror server at the Call Center, and that the Call Center users are also present as users at each remote center. In addition, the Call Center must have the "Call Center" Conqueror Pro license while each remote center must have at least one "Remote Access" license.

Template letters for reservation confirmations and booking confirmation e-mails are sent automatically from Conqueror without passing through the local mail client on the Call Center Conqueror terminal. The Consolidated Reports feature allows reservation reports to be easily obtained from any/all remote centers without needing to access each center individually.

Terminal and operator user preferences on the local Call Center terminals are preserved when connecting to the remote centers, Call Center terminals are able to connect to remote centers with different Conqueror versions; a Conqueror user privilege controls which Call Center users have access to Authorization Required centers through a privilege; and it is possible to set remote centers as Not Available for call center operators in the case of problems at the remote center.
