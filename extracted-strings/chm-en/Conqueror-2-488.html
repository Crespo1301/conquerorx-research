9.4 High Scores - Settings Tab

To view or print a High Score, open the Standings tab (Front Desk> Lane Control> High Scores). The last five intervals of Daily, Weekly and Monthly High Scores are always accessible (e.g. the last five days of the Daily High Scores, including the current day). In the case of custom High Scores, all defined rankings are accessible, while the All-Time ranking is, by definition, only one.
The "Center information header" checkbox allows including or excluding the center header from the printing, which is typically in all Conqueror reports. The Modify button allows changing players' names. To do modify the name of "dummy" players use the "Default Players" button.
