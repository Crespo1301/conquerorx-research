6.3 Linking Price Keys to Modifiers

To do this, click on the Price Keys page and press the Price Keys button.

To associate all the modifiers of the group altogether:

      1. Click on the modifier group on the left-hand side,
      2. click on the Price Keys tab,
      3. press the Price Keys button: the whole price keys structure opens and it is possible to choose all the price keys to associate to the whole group of modifiers.

To associate only one modifier (not the whole group):

      1. Click on the modifier group,
      2. click on the modifier in the list on the right,
      3. press the Price Keys button: the whole price keys structure opens and it is possible to choose all the price keys to associate to the selected modifier.

To unlink a price key from a modifier select the price key in the list and press Unlink Price Keys .
