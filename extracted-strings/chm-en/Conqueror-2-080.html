6.6 Voiding a Lane Opening

If errors are made when opening a lane, it may be easier to void the lane opening rather than correct the errors in the modification window, depending on the errors. Void can only be applied if articles have not been utilized or paid for, i.e. players have not yet begun to bowl and POS items have not yet been bought, otherwise these items must be refunded. To void an entire lane, go to the Lane Status module, select the lane, press the Void button and confirm. Any voids performed are included in the financial reports.
