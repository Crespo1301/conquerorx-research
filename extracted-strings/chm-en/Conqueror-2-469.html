5. Externals

These external programs work together with the Conqueror system to provide additional useful services.

Click on the boxes on the left-hand side to select the folder to which the QuickBooks Desktop , Year & QCad and Bar files can be exported.

In the section on the right-hand side, click on the boxes to select the following executable files (.exe): QuickBooks Desktop , BLS and BTM.
