5.6 Undoing Payments

POS > Reprint Receipts

From this window it is also possible to:

  • undo a payment in order to change the payment mode after the receipt has been issued,
  • reopen a bill closed by mistake.

Select the receipt to be undone and press Undo : a new receipt is printed, voiding the payment one, and the tab is displayed in the Payment window, ready to be corrected and paid.

The Reprint Receipt module is also accessible directly from the Sales window through the Reprint button, but only by operators having the Undo Payment User Privilege.

Restrictions

At present it is not possible to undo: credit card payments, debit card payments, payments made through Frequent Bowler accounts, payments involving packages with open choice menus, refunds.
