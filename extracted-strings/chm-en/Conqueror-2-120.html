17.2 Reporting

Back Office > Reporting > Statistical Reports

In the Redemption statistical reports, it is necessary to choose:

  • a time period
  • a hypothetical value of the ticket
  • a hypothetical percentage proportion Tickets/Income.

The report will display how, in the period selected, the actual figures differ from those expected. This will allow the manager to adjust the settings in order to obtain the desired results.

LANE MANAGEMENT > Redemption Ticket Management > Reporting
