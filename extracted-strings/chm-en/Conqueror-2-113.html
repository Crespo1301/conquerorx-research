15.9 Classic Games

This title refers to alternative bowling games.

It is possible to identify the lanes on which these games are run through the colored icon in Lane Status.

Supplementary rules: when a Split Converted is scored, a Strike is attributed and when a Split is scored, a Spare is attributed.

3 6 9

Here the 3rd, 6th and 9th frames are not played as players are automatically attributed three Strikes.

With BES, Bowland and Bowland-X, the 3-6-9 default can be changed to any other frame, by using the buttons 1-9.

Even Better

One ball per frame at a full rack

In this game, the player must bowl an even number of pins, but not a strike.

If the player knocks down an even number of pins, his score is normally recorded.

If the player knocks down an odd number of pins or a strike or a gutter, the score will be zero.

The maximum score per game is 80.

Head Hunter

One ball per frame at a full rack - 10 frames

The object is to hit the head pin without getting a strike.

If you don't hit the head pin or scores a strike, the score will be zero.

The 1st ball of each frame is displayed and managed as a gutter (it counts zero and it does not add to the previous frame, in case it was a spare).

To score, the ball must hit the head pin, and your score will be how many pins you knock down.

If you hit 7, 6, 5 or 4 pins, you get a bonus that converts your score to 10 (displayed as a spare).

The maximum score is 100.

Last Pin Standing

Two ball at one rack - maximum

The object is to knock down as many pins without hitting the head pin.

If you knock down pins (but not head pin) on the 1st ball, you can throw the 2nd ball.

If the 2nd ball knocks down the head pin, hits no pin or is a gutter ball, the player receives for that frame only the score of his 1st ball.

If the player throws two balls, knocking down pins every time, but without hitting the head pin he receives his score plus 3 point bonus.

If all pins (except the head pin) fall down on the 1st ball, the player gets a strike and has 5 point bonus; in this case the 2nd ball will not be thrown.

If you hit all pins but the head with the 2nd bowl, you get a spare.

In the case of a gutter (or head pin 1 fallen), the frame is over (if it occurs on the 2nd ball, the score for the frame will be what done on 1st ball)

The maximum score per game is 300.

Low Game

The aim of this game is to have the lowest possible score without throwing the ball into the gutter. It is therefore necessary that the players try to knock down only one pin with each throw (the lowest score possible is 20).

The players must avoid the gutters on the first bowl because a Gutter throw is recognized as a Strike; on the second bowl a Gutter is recognized as a Spare, while all other scores are counted as normal (e.g. Strike = Strike, 3= 3, etc.).

Fouls are managed as gutters as well.

No Tap

If the first bowl in the frame scores 7 or more, 8 or more or 9 or more (according to the settings), it is converted to a Strike.

Odd Duck

One ball per frame at a full rack

In this game, the player must bowl an odd number of pins, and not a strike.

If the player knocks down an odd number of pins, his score is normally recorded.

If the player knocks down an even number of pins or a strike or a gutter, the score will be zero.

The maximum score per game is 90.

Odds and Evens

In this game, the player must bowl an even number of pins to be automatically attributed a Strike. When an odd number of pins is bowled, the player is automatically attributed a Spare on the second frame, regardless of the actual number of pins knocked down.
