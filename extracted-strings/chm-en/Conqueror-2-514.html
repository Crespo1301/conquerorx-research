6.2 Creating a Modifier

First press Add Group , then give the modifier group a name (e.g. Sauces ) and specify the maximum number of modifiers that can be chosen from that particular modifier group (e.g. no more than 1 degree of cooking and 2 sauces).

This option avoids the waiters from making mistakes (e.g. adding the degree of cooking twice) and also speeds up their work because when the requested choices have been made, the system automatically prompts for the next group of modifiers. E.g. when the waiter has added the 2 sauces allowed, the system automatically prompts for the degree of cooking.

In order to not limit the modifier choices, set it to 0.

When the group is created, press Add Modifier and give the modifier a name (e.g. Ketchup ), a price and a tax. After inserting all the modifiers, it is necessary to link the price keys to the modifiers. In this case all the price keys that might request a sauce have to be associated to the Sauces modifier group.

During the setup phase, a price and a tax can be added to each modifier.
