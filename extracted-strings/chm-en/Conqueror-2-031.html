2.4 Sending Orders to Kitchen Printer

For the price key sub-departments pertaining to the F&B main department it is possible to set a specific printer (or printer profile), so that orders are sent to it, e.g. the kitchen item department has to be associated to the kitchen printer so that kitchen orders will be printed on the printer set here.

The price key sent to the kitchen printer are those set as Kitchen Printable in the Price Key Setup.

The printers available in the scroll down menu are the printers installed on the Conqueror Server computer.

POINT OF SALE > Sales > Sending Orders to Kitchen Printer
