10.5 Sending an Item to the Lane

To send an item to a lane, select the item, press Open , choose the lanes from the window that opens and press OK . In the Lane Control module that opens, the opening can be performed. The item is then automatically removed from the waiting list. If opening a lane from Lane Status when there is an item present in the waiting list, the following warning appears: Warning, the waiting list is not empty! . The operator can then decide whether to open the item in the waiting list or ignore the warning by pressing Cancel and proceed with the opening.
