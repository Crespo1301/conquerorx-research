19.3 Sequence Item Setup

Double click on any sequence to open the Sequence Item Setup window either to modify Lane Lights Effects or Capping Lights Effects.

Modifying Lane Lights and Capping lights

Select an effect from the Lane Lights list to modify:

      • From /To Color : Define the color range that you want to rotate (From) and the color range you want to rotate (To). Double click on the color to open the color palette.
      • Speed : Change Speed of the effect, open the dropdown menu and select your speed.
      • Direction: Change the direction of the effect, from left to right or vice-versa.
      • Duration: Edit the duration of the sequence, just add the duration in the Duration box
      • Size: Change the size of the colored section on the bumpers.
      • Delay: Change the delay value to modify the color transition.
      • Deck lights only: The effect runs only on deck lights.
      • Deck + bumper light: The effect runs on deck and bumpers lights.

Please note: some features are available for limited effects only.
