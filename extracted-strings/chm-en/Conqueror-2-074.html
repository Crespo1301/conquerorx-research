5.9 Opening a Booked Lane

If a lane has been reserved through the Booking System, when nearing the booking time, the lane icon displays the opening time of that lane. If trying to open this lane nearing the booked time, a warning message appears, whereby the operator decides whether to start the reservation or proceed to open the lane for other customers. If trying to open a lane when there are customers on the Waiting List, the "Warning, the waiting list is not empty!" message is displayed, whereby the operator decides whether to ignore this warning by pressing Cancel and proceed to open the lane, or open the item on the waiting list on this lane.
